Who We Are

A Sanctuary Born From Passion

Widelife Sanctuary was founded with a single vision — to create a safe haven where wildlife can thrive, ecosystems heal, and nature finds its voice again.

100% Protected Zone
Dense Forest Cover

12,000+ acres of protected forest

Wildlife Protection

Our rangers patrol 24/7 to ensure every animal within our boundaries remains safe from poaching and habitat loss.

Ecosystem Restoration

We actively restore rivers, wetlands, and grasslands to their natural state, creating thriving habitats for diverse species.

Education & Awareness

Through guided tours, school programs, and research partnerships, we inspire the next generation of conservationists.

Global Partnerships

We collaborate with international wildlife organizations and governments to advance conservation laws and policies.
